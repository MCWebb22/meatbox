# Coverage: what is in the sim, and why

Generated by `java -cp out meatbox.Coverage` from `src/meatbox/Coverage.java`. Do not edit by hand.

Two published lists decide what goes in. The **GBD 2021 cause list** (IHME's Global Burden of Disease study) has 305 leaf causes of illness, injury and death, and together they cover every ICD-10 code. The **DSM-5-TR** (American Psychiatric Association, 2022) names every recognised mental disorder. The catalogue holds 550 conditions.

- GBD causes with at least one sim condition: 292 of 305. The rest are folded into other rows, or need a system that is not there yet (drugs, transport, a state).
- DSM-5-TR disorders with a sim condition: 114 of 119.
- "Default world" says whether the condition can happen in the stock temperate village. Turn a switch on in `Config.java` (for example `E_AEDES` for dengue) to bring it in.
- *folded*: counted inside the named conditions. *hook*: the behaviour or social code gives the condition when the event happens. *n/a*: cannot happen here, with the reason.
